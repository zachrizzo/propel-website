# Propel — 30-second product walkthrough

## Audience and promise

For job seekers who are tired of re-entering the same information. The video
shows that Propel turns their existing profile into real browser work while
keeping them in control of every submission.

## Recording requirements

- Record the desktop app and Chrome as separate, clean, full-screen masters.
- Use a pre-existing or prepared application flow; do not send a real job
  application while filming.
- Do not show address, phone number, email, passwords, or unrelated desktop
  windows.
- Use Zach's `zach 2` voice clone for the supplied 30-second script.

## QA gate before delivery

1. Watch the completed video from start to finish at normal speed.
2. Check every narration line against the action visible at that moment.
3. Inspect frames at every cut and every zoom for clipping, empty screens,
   desktop leakage, notifications, and readability.
4. Verify 30-second runtime, intelligible voiceover, and no accidental real
   submission or personal information.
